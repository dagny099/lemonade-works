# saturday market vendor packet notes

- vendor setup is 7:00–7:45 am. cars out of the lane by 7:50.
- booth fee is $20 each saturday, due before opening.
- food vendors need the city temporary food permit at the booth. keep a copy where they can see it.
- sign has to show the business name and prices. letters at least 1 inch. no signs in the walking aisle.
- booth space is 10 x 10. nothing past the tape.
- market hours 8–12. dont break down early unless weather call.
